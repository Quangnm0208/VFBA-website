## What passed (all 48 lines of the log)

- Form API (23 lines): methods, content types, oversize, malformed JSON, foreign/missing Origin, honeypot, too-fast (429, never a false "saved"), invalid e-mail, missing consent, newsletter opt-in and version, missing Turnstile token, valid submissions (200), duplicate address with another letter case (no second row), rate limit (429 within 25 requests, per address not per form).
- CRM anonymous: login page 200; every protected page 401 and no data; headers (private no-store, noindex, DENY framing, same-origin referrer policy, script-free CSP, never a CDN hit); anonymous POST 401; cross-origin POST 403.
- CRM sign-in with the **real** Supabase Auth: staff sign-in 303 with three hardened cookies; a valid Auth user who is not on the allowlist gets the same answer as a wrong password; wrong password 401 and no cookie.
- The three leads created by the form tests appear in the inbox; the membership lead has a detail link; the detail shows consent version `join-v2`, the consent time and the source page from the real row.
- Scope: a newsletter-only person sees the newsletter leads and not the membership lead; IDOR: the same person gets 404 for the membership lead on read, status change and note.
- RBAC: staff cannot open staff management (403); an administrator can (200).
- Workflow: New → In progress → Closed through the real database; a stale form is a conflict, not an overwrite; an internal note is stored with author, time and line break.
- Pages carry no script, inline style or handler.
- Logout: 303 and all three cookies expired; **a cookie copied before logout no longer works (Supabase revoked the session): 401**; forged tokens and a hand-edited session clock: 401.
- Sign-in throttle: nine guesses from one address end in 429 (real database counters).
